How I can support your production

Every production is different, but sustainability can be integrated into every stage of the filmmaking process.

My role is to provide practical support from soft-prep to post-production, adapting solutions to each project's needs.

The sooner we start working together the most we can do.

Pre-production

Building a strong sustainability strategy before the first shooting day makes it easier to reduce environmental impact without compromising creativity or production needs.

Identifying practical opportunities within the script to reduce environmental impact while respecting the creative vision.
Developing a tailored sustainability plan aligned with the production's goals.
Researching suppliers, services and alternatives that support more sustainable production choices.
Supporting productions in preparing documentation and requirements in case you want to apply for any green certification

Production

Sustainability works best when it becomes part of everyday production. Practical collaboration across departments helps turn planning into real action on set.

Supporting the implementation of sustainability actions throughout production.
Collecting environmental data and tracking sustainability performance.
Working alongside departments to integrate practical sustainability solutions.
Finding realistic alternatives whenever new challenges arise during production.

Post-production

Every production generates valuable knowledge. Measuring results today helps build more sustainable productions tomorrow.

Preparing a final report summarizing actions, achievements and measurable results.
Providing practical and useful recommendations for your future productions.
Supporting the completion of sustainability and certification documentation.

My journey into sustainable filmmaking

My interest in sustainable filmmaking started on set. Working in different roles on set, gave me a first-hand understanding of how film sets operate and where sustainability can make the greatest difference.

Seeing the challenges of everyday production from the inside inspired me to specialize as an sustainability manager. My professional journey combines specialised training in sustainable audiovisual production with hands-on experience in film environments.
